Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a plain-language starting policy for a small, early-stage business. It is not a substitute for legal advice — before you rely on it, especially as you take on more clients, have a lawyer review it against Nigeria's Data Protection Act/NDPR and any other laws that apply where your clients live.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and the plan you choose. If you sign up for the free trial, we also record the IP address your signup came from, used only to spot repeat trial sign-ups. Once approved, we collect the business profile information you provide (your role, target audience, brand voice, platforms you use), and whatever details you submit for each job (product, offer or project information, content requests, and any files you upload). If you leave a review, we collect your rating and comment.

Payments

Subscription payments are processed by Paystack. We do not see or store your card details — Paystack handles that directly and securely. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, along with a subscription reference we use to manage your access and billing dates.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (which pages are viewed, general location, device type). Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. You can opt out using your browser's tracking protection settings or an ad blocker, and the site works fine either way. This site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your browser's IP address as part of loading those files.

How we use it

We use this information to review and manage your account, produce the content you request, process payments, communicate with you about your jobs, and understand how people use the site so we can improve it. We do not sell your information to third parties.

How it's processed

Your account, business profile, job details, and files are stored using Supabase, a third-party database and file storage provider. We do not run our own servers — this site is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages, with Supabase handling login, data storage, and file uploads securely on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and job information for as long as your account is active, or until you delete it yourself from your dashboard, or ask us to delete it. Deleting your account permanently removes your login, business profile, job history, and files from our systems.

Your choices

You can access, update, or delete most of your own information directly from your dashboard at any time, including permanently deleting your account. For anything else, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing trevqioagency@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to trevqioagency@gmail.com.
