Questions

Clear answers. No surprises.

Do you work with businesses outside Nigeria?

Yes. Trevqio is designed to serve small businesses in Nigeria and internationally. Pricing currency and communication arrangements are agreed before work starts.

Do I need to give you my passwords?

No. For managed accounts, use official platform permissions where available. Never send passwords in a chat or intake form.

What does the 7-day free trial include?

The trial uses a small, written scope—typically two posts with captions. It does not include unlimited content, paid ads or ongoing customer support.

Do you guarantee followers or sales?

No. We focus on reliable content delivery and agreed management tasks. Results depend on many factors and are reported honestly.
