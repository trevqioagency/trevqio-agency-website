Common questions

Good to know before you start.

If your business needs something outside these packages, contact us with the brief and we’ll clarify what is feasible.

Ask a question
Do you only work with Nigerian businesses?

No. Trevqio is based in Nigeria and welcomes suitable small-business clients in Nigeria and internationally. We confirm time zones, currency and scope before work begins.

Do I need to know how to use AI?

No. We may use AI-assisted tools as part of our workflow, but you provide the business context and review the final deliverables. AI does not replace your approval.

Will you post directly to my social accounts?

Publishing support is included only in the social media management packages. We agree on platforms and access first, and content should be approved before publishing. Never send account passwords by email or chat.

What do you need from me?

Usually your business name, offer or service details, target audience, preferred tone, logo or brand colours if available, and any photos or footage you want us to use.

Is the 7-day trial completely free?

The trial request has no payment requirement and is limited to two post concepts, two matching captions and one simple content direction, subject to confirming the brief. It does not include ongoing publishing, ad spend or inbox management.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Tell us your platforms, content volume, publishing needs and budget. We will confirm whether the work fits our capacity and provide a written scope before you commit.
