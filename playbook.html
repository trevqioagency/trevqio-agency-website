Master Client Context (run once per client, first thing)

You are the AI content-production assistant for Trevqio Agency, a marketing agency that helps small businesses show up consistently on social media. Your task is to create accurate, professional and engaging marketing content based ONLY on information provided by the client. CLIENT INFORMATION: Name: [Client Name] Business: [Business Name] Industry: [e.g. restaurant, salon, retail, local service] Market/Location: [City, State, Country] TARGET AUDIENCE: [Target Audience] BRAND VOICE: [Brand Voice — e.g. Professional / Friendly / Modern / Bold / Educational] BUSINESS GOALS: [e.g. Get more customers / Build brand awareness / Save time / Promote an offer] PLATFORMS: [Instagram / Facebook / TikTok / etc.] IMPORTANT RULES: 1. Never invent products, prices, offers, dates, statistics, testimonials or claims. 2. Never present assumptions as facts. 3. Follow applicable advertising rules in the client's market. Make no health, income or results guarantees, and no misleading before/after or comparison claims unless the client has supplied evidence. 4. Avoid discriminatory or exclusionary language of any kind. 5. Keep the content professional and clear. 6. Include a relevant call-to-action where appropriate. 7. Adapt the writing to the client's market and audience. Confirm you understand this client profile and are ready to create content.

Day 1 — Welcome & Business Review

No AI content today — manual review + a short client message.

  • Read their submitted business profile and job details
  • Skim their website/social accounts if given
  • Confirm the written trial scope with the client (what's included, what's not)
  • Confirm you have the brand assets (logo, colours, photos) you need to start
Hi [Name], welcome to your Trevqio trial! We've reviewed your business and what you'd like to promote, and we're starting your content today. Your first pieces will land in your dashboard over the next few days. The trial covers [agreed scope, e.g. 2 branded posts with captions].

Day 2 — Social Media Posts

Create the social media posts agreed for this client's trial, using the client information already established. Focus for this job: [Product / offer / project name] — [Type] Key details to include (use only what's listed, never invent): [Key details] Price (if given): [Price] | Dates (if given): [Start] to [End] | Link: [Link] For each post give: headline, main text, call-to-action, recommended platform, and a suggested visual idea for the branded graphic. Don't invent facts or statistics. Make every post distinct — avoid repeating hooks or phrases across posts. Things to avoid: [Avoid, if any]

Day 3 — Captions

Create captions to match the posts from Day 2, based on the client information already established. Vary the style where the scope allows: Professional, Educational, Conversational, and Engagement-focused. Each caption needs a strong opening hook, clear concise content, and a natural call-to-action. Include hashtags (5–8) only if the client's platform and voice suit them. Keep them authentic. Don't use excessive emojis. Don't make unsupported claims. Things to avoid: [Avoid, if any]

Day 4 — Branded Graphics

Build the graphics for the Day 2 posts using the client's own brand assets (logo, colours, fonts, photos). If the client hasn't supplied brand assets, ask for them now rather than inventing a look.

  • Logo, colours and fonts match the client's brand
  • Text on each graphic matches the approved post copy exactly
  • Sized correctly for the agreed platform(s)
  • Only photos/images the client supplied or has the right to use
Write a short, plain-language brief for each graphic (layout, headline text, supporting text, image to use, colours from the brand) so it can be built in the design tool. Use only the client's supplied brand details and the approved Day 2 copy.

Day 6 — Review & Revisions Prep

  • Re-read every piece against the client's original details
  • Fix tone mismatches and repeated hooks
  • Confirm graphics and captions pair up correctly
  • Run everything through the QC checklist below

Quality Control — check every piece before it goes to a client

Accuracy

  • No invented information
  • Prices, dates and offers are exactly as the client gave them
  • Business name, names and locations are spelled correctly

Brand

  • Matches the client's tone
  • Uses the client's real brand assets
  • Suitable for the target audience

Marketing

  • Clear hook
  • Clear call-to-action
  • Not repetitive across pieces

Compliance

  • No discriminatory or exclusionary language
  • No misleading claims
  • No unsupported guarantees (health, income, results)

Never send raw AI output straight to a client — this checklist is the gate between draft and delivery.
